Host the TurboGames folder, then add it to your Google Site with an embed. This helper works on your computer, too.

  1. Put TurboGames online

    Extract the download. Upload the folder containing index.html to Netlify Drop, or another static website host. Copy its HTTPS website address.

  2. Make your embed below

    Paste the website address into this helper. Use the address of your deployed TurboGames site.

  3. Add it to Google Sites

    Open your site editor. Choose Insert → Embed → Embed code, paste the code, then choose Next → Insert. Make the embed wide and tall, then publish your Google Site.

Use the public HTTPS site address from your host.

Open your arcade ↗

Google Sites embeds a hosted website; it cannot run the downloaded folder or ZIP itself. Some games or fullscreen controls may be limited inside Google Sites. The included “Open TurboGames in a new tab” link opens the full arcade.

Game loading can still depend on the game’s original online assets. Keep the complete TurboGames folder together when you upload it.
